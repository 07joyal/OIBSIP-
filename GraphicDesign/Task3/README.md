# Graphic Design Internship – Task 3: Business Card Design

## 📌 Project Title
**CAPSI BELLEI – Business Card Design**

## 🎯 Objective
Design a professional and visually appealing business card that reflects the visual identity of the CAPSI BELLEI brand while maintaining clarity, readability, and brand consistency.

---

## 🏢 Brand Concept

The business card was designed for the fictional brand **CAPSI BELLEI**, building upon the logo created in Task 2.

The design follows a clean and minimalist style, using the brand's signature green color palette and modern typography. The goal was to create a professional identity piece that effectively communicates brand information while maintaining a strong visual presence.

---

## 🛠 Design Process

- Incorporated the CAPSI BELLEI logo as the primary branding element.
- Designed a double-sided business card layout.
- Created a visually impactful front side focused on brand identity.
- Organized contact details clearly on the back side.
- Maintained consistency with the brand color palette and typography.
- Ensured the design was suitable for both digital display and print production.

---

## 🎨 Card Layout

### Front Side
- CAPSI BELLEI logo placed prominently at the center.
- Brand name displayed using bold typography.
- Founder and Creative Director designation highlighted.
- Dark green background to strengthen brand recognition.

### Back Side
- Contact information arranged in a clean and structured format.
- Includes:
  - Phone Number
  - Email Address
  - Website
  - Instagram Handle
  - LinkedIn Profile
- White background for enhanced readability.
- Logo repeated to maintain visual consistency.
