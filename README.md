# Graphic Design Internship - Task 2: Logo Design

## Project Title

CAPSI BELLEI - Logo Design

## Objective

Design a unique and memorable logo that represents a brand through simple shapes, typography, and visual identity principles.

## Brand Concept

The logo was created for a fictional brand named **CAPSI BELLEI**.

The design is inspired by the natural shape of a bell pepper (capsicum). The logo combines geometric circles and simplified organic forms to create a distinctive symbol that reflects nature, freshness, creativity, and modern design.

## Design Process

1. Studied the structure and symmetry of a bell pepper.
2. Converted the natural form into simplified geometric shapes.
3. Created a clean vector-style symbol.
4. Developed multiple logo variations for different branding requirements.
5. Selected a bold sans-serif typeface to improve readability and brand recognition.

## Logo Variations

### 1. Monochrome Version

Used for black-and-white printing, documents, and minimal branding.

### 2. Reversed Version

White logo on a dark green background for dark-theme applications.

### 3. Primary Brand Version

Green logo on a light background representing freshness, growth, and sustainability.

## Color Palette

* Green (#0B7A3B)
* White (#FFFFFF)
* Black (#000000)

## Design Features

* Geometric construction
* Scalable vector-style appearance
* High readability
* Minimalist design approach
* Multiple branding variations
* Suitable for print and digital media

# Graphic Design Internship - Task 4: Infographic Design

## Objective

Design an informative infographic that presents complex information in a visually engaging and easy-to-understand format while applying principles of visual hierarchy, typography, color consistency, and data visualization.

## Project Overview

This task focuses on creating an educational infographic titled **"Evolution of Earth – Key Facts Across 4.5 Billion Years"**. The infographic visually represents major milestones in Earth's history using a timeline-based layout and concise informational content.

### Evolution of Earth Infographic

The infographic illustrates significant events in Earth's development, beginning with its formation approximately 4.54 billion years ago and progressing through major evolutionary milestones. A structured timeline design is used to improve readability and chronological understanding.

## Key Topics Covered

* Molten Beginnings (~4.54 Billion Years Ago)
* Birth of the Moon (~4.5 Billion Years Ago)
* First Life in Oceans (>3.5 Billion Years Ago)
* Oxygen Revolution (~2.4 Billion Years Ago)
* Age of the Dinosaurs (230–66 Million Years Ago)
* Bonus Fact: Earth's History on a 24-Hour Scale

## Design Features Implemented

* Timeline-based infographic layout
* Consistent color palette and visual theme
* Clear information hierarchy
* High-contrast and readable typography
* Icon-based visual elements
* Chronological data visualization
* Educational and informative content presentation
